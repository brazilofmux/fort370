FORTLIB's speed is assembler in registers, one argument reduction in
long precision and a short polynomial or rational function, nothing
carried past 14 hex digits: within a unit or two where the reduction
is exact or small (DSQRT, DEXP, DATAN, DASIN), but the trigonometric
functions lose a digit for every power of 16 in x, and X**Y is off
in its last digit five times in six.
